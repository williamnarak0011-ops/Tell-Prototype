TELL

You are Lulu. The Yeti is played by the computer. Each turn you both pick one skill in secret, then both play out at the same time.

  • Faster moves hit first and cancel slower ones.
  • Read the Yeti: its mood (glow and the hint over its head) tells you what it's allowed to do and how it will fight.
  • Click the Yeti's skills (bottom right) to see where they hit.
Full rules
  • Frames: every skill has Startup (frames before it can hit), Active (frames it can hit) and Recovery. Both moves run side by side, frame by frame, so a fast move can land before a slow one ever comes out. Hit someone during their Startup and their move is cancelled.
  • Counter-hit: get hit during your own attack's Startup or Active frames and you lose 10 Sanity. The one who landed it gains 5.
  • Combo: at 15 hits in a row, and every 5 after, the attacker gains 5 Sanity and the defender loses 5.
  • Sanity sets each fighter's emotion, and every emotion has a passive that limits moves. Click a fighter (or their name) to read it.
  • Light pays for specials. You get 1 back each turn, up to 3.
  • Getting hit leaves you Staggered: +4 frames on your attacks next turn. Moving or guarding shakes it off (no penalty). Ice attacks leave you Chilled: +4 frames on everything.
  • Parry (YOMI style): choose High or Low and the frame you expect the hit. Right frame + right height = Perfect Parry: no damage, +1 Light, and the attacker is Staggered. Anything else is a block: 1/3 chip damage, more if the height is wrong. Low attacks need Low; mid and high attacks need High. If you parried last turn and nothing came, you can only block.
  • Grab (YOMI throws): pick Forward or Back Throw, optionally as a Dash Grab (+4 frames, lunges in). An active throw beats blocks, parries, armor and any strike landing on the same frame, but a strike that lands first stops it. You can't throw someone airborne, dashing, jumping or rolling, already hit this turn, or who blocked a hit. Both grabbing within 9 frames = Throw Tech.
  • Burst: one stock each, refills over time and when you take damage. Defensive while being comboed (15 invulnerable frames, resets the combo) or Offensive right after you land a hit.
  • Stances: For Justice! changes your stance, so the button becomes [2], then [3]. Lunge and Joust only work in Normal stance. Counter-hits do ×1.1 damage. Roll can't be hit while rolling.
  • The Yeti remembers the moves you use most and starts picking moves that beat them. Using the same attack 3 turns in a row makes it Predictable: +3 frames.
  • The faint shape shows where your move goes. The box shows where your attack hits. Click any Yeti skill to see its frames and its hit box on the stage.
  • Keys: Q W E R move · A Lunge · S For Justice! · D Grab · Z Parry (↑/↓ height, ←/→ frame) · X Joust · C Burst · ←/→ distance · Enter lock in · Esc cancel.
